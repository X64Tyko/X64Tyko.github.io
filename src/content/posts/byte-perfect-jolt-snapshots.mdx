## The rate mismatch

{/* TODO. The genuinely interesting structural problem, and the one most
    rollback writing skips because it assumes a single rate.

      - Logic runs at 512Hz, physics at 64Hz. Eight logic ticks per physics
        step.
      - So a rollback target usually does not land on a physics snapshot, and
        the worst case is 7 frames of physics approximated forward from the
        nearest one.
      - At 64Hz that is roughly 109ms of approximation. Whether that is
        acceptable is a judgement about what players perceive, so make the
        judgement explicitly rather than implying it is free.
      - The ring depth floor of 8 exists precisely to cover this divisor. */}
