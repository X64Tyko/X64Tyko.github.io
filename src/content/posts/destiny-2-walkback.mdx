## The part that does not survive the reversal

{/* TODO: the sharpest thing available, so give it room.

    The vaulting was presented as effectively permanent and technically
    necessary. Six years on it is being undone. Either the permanence was
    always a choice dressed as a constraint, or the constraint was real and is
    now being paid down anyway because the business needs it to be. Bungie's
    own framing points at the second: they say lighting, encounters, combatants
    and underlying systems all need reworking before any of it can return.

    Which raises the question the piece exists to ask. If it is genuinely that
