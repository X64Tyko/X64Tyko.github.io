{/* STUB, reframed 2026-09-25.

    The earlier version of this was titled "The Mod Scene as Bug Report" and
    argued that a large mod scene is product feedback. That overclaims badly.
    Most modding is not repair: it is expression, extension, personalisation,
    and people making things for a game they like. Treating the whole scene as
    a complaint gets modding wrong and is also faintly insulting to it.

    So the piece narrows. The fix-shaped subset is real and worth writing
    about; it just isn't representative, and the article has to say so early or
    a modder will say it for you. */}
