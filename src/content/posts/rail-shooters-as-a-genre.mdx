## What the rail buys

{/* TODO
      - Authored pacing. Beats land when the designer says, not when the player
        wanders into them.
      - Framing. Every set piece can be composed, because the camera is known.
      - Difficulty that means something. If everyone crosses the same space at
        the same speed, encounter tuning is a real curve rather than a guess.
      - Readability at speed. Attention goes to threats instead of navigation. */}

## What it costs

{/* TODO. Concede properly and early.
      - No exploration, and no player-authored pace.
      - Replay has to come from scoring, routes or branches, because it cannot
        come from the space itself.
      - A bad section cannot be avoided. On rails, tedium is mandatory. */}
